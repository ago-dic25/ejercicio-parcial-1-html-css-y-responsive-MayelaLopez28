Spider-Man

Your Friendly Neighborhood Hero

Peter Parker is a regular teenager who got bitten by a radioactive spider. Now he has super powers and fights bad guys in New York City. He can climb walls, swing on webs, and has super strength. Spider-Man always tries to help people and keep the city safe.

Spider Powers

Spider-Man is super strong and fast. He can lift heavy things and jump really high. He also has great reflexes and can move very quickly to avoid danger.

Web Shooting

Peter made special web shooters that help him swing around the city. His webs are very strong and sticky. He uses them to catch bad guys and travel fast.

Spider Sense

Spider-Man has a special feeling called "spider sense". It helps him know when danger is coming. This power keeps him safe and helps him save other people.
